# Text to Image

Type what you see in your mind, and watch it become a picture.  
This is your creative starting point — whether you're sketching ideas, designing characters, or visualizing a scene that doesn't exist yet.

---

## 🪄 When to Use It

Use Text to Image when you're starting from scratch — no reference photos, just an idea.

It works great for:
- **Concept art** and mood boards
- **Character designs** and portraits
- **Product mockups** before prototyping
- **Environments** and landscapes for storytelling

**Skill level:** Beginner  
**Average time:** 3–8 seconds per image  
**Cost:** Low

---

## 🎚️ Controls and Parameters

### **Prompt** (Text)
What you want to create. Be specific about mood, style, lighting, and composition.

💡 **Tip:** Try "A cozy coffee shop interior, warm lighting, vintage furniture, cinematic" instead of just "coffee shop."

### **Styles** (Optional)
Import custom LoRA files trained on specific visual styles (watercolor, anime, cinematic, etc.) to guide the aesthetic of your generation.

⚠️ **Note:** Styles only work with **Flux models** (dev, SRPO, Krea, Chroma) and **SDXL**. Not compatible with Nano Banana or GPT Image.

💡 **Tip:** Train custom style LoRAs on platforms like Civitai, Replicate, or Fal.ai, then import the `.safetensors` file. Built-in training coming soon!

**How to use in prompt:**
```
A beautiful landscape <lora:watercolor_style:1.0>
```

### **Character** (Optional)
Import custom LoRA files trained on specific characters or faces to maintain consistent appearance across multiple generations.

⚠️ **Note:** Characters only work with **Flux models** (dev, SRPO, Krea, Chroma) and **SDXL**. Not compatible with Nano Banana or GPT Image.

💡 **Tip:** Train character LoRAs using 10-20 images of your character on Civitai, Replicate, or Fal.ai. Import the `.safetensors` file to use in Wundernode.

**How to use in prompt:**
```
Portrait of a hero <lora:my_character:1.0>
```

**LoRA strength control:**
- `1.0` — full strength (recommended starting point)
- `0.5` — subtle influence, blended with base model
- `1.5` — stronger effect (may override prompt details)

### **Control Maps** (Optional, Advanced)
Upload reference images to guide composition, depth, or style:
- **Control 1, 2, 3:** Add depth maps, edge guides, or pose references
- **Strength (0–1):** How closely to follow the control image

💡 **Tip:** Start without control maps until you need precise composition control.

## 🎨 Available Models

Different AI models offer different styles and capabilities. Choose the one that fits your needs:

### **Nano Banana** (Default)
Fast, general-purpose model. Great for quick iterations and testing ideas.

### **GPT Image**
Optimized for realistic images with flexible aspect ratios and transparent backgrounds.

### **Flux [dev]**
High-quality model with excellent prompt following. Supports control maps for advanced composition control.

### **SDXL**
Multiple sub-models available (Juggernaut, Night Vision, Epic Realism, etc.) — each with distinct visual styles.

### **Flux [SRPO]**
Enhanced Flux variant with improved realism and detail preservation.

### **Flux [Krea]**
Creative variant of Flux optimized for artistic and stylized outputs.

### **Chroma [V50]**
Advanced color and lighting control for professional-grade results.

### **Seedream V4**
Latest model with support for ultra-high resolution (up to 4K) and various aspect ratios.

💡 **Tip:** Start with Nano Banana for speed, switch to Flux or Seedream for final high-quality renders.

## 🎨 What to Expect

Your first result might not be perfect — and that's normal.  
Think of this node as a sketch tool. You can:
- Regenerate with a different seed
- Adjust your prompt for clarity
- Pass the result to other nodes for refinement

Text to Image gives you a solid foundation to build on.

---

## 💬 Quick Tips

- Be descriptive but not overwhelming — 1-2 sentences work best
- Mention lighting and composition early in your prompt
- Try different styles to find what matches your vision
- Save your favorite prompts for reuse
