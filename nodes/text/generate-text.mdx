# Generate Text

Turn ideas into words.  
This node uses AI language models (like GPT-5 or Gemini) to generate text based on your prompts. Perfect for brainstorming concepts, writing descriptions, creating dialogue, or expanding on ideas before turning them into images or videos.

---

## 🪄 When to Use It

Use Generate Text when you need to develop ideas, write copy, or create content.

It works great for:
- **Prompt generation** — create detailed image prompts from simple ideas
- **Copywriting** — product descriptions, captions, ad copy
- **Dialogue writing** — character conversations for videos
- **Brainstorming** — explore concepts before visualizing them
- **Story development** — flesh out scenes and narratives

**Skill level:** Beginner  
**Average time:** 5–15 seconds  
**Cost:** Very Low

---

## 🎚️ Controls and Parameters

### **Prompts** (Array, Optional)
A series of text instructions or questions for the AI. Can be:
- A single prompt: "Write a product description for eco-friendly headphones"
- Multiple prompts: Build context by chaining instructions
- Conversation: Ask follow-up questions

💡 **Tip:** Be specific. "Write a 50-word product description for wireless headphones targeting creative professionals" is better than "write about headphones."

### **Images** (Array, Optional)
Upload images for the AI to analyze or reference in its response.

💡 **Tip:** Use this to generate image descriptions, extract text from images, or get creative prompts based on visual references.

---

## 🎨 Available Models

Choose the AI model that fits your task:

### **Gemini 2.5 Pro** (Default)
Google's most capable model. Excellent for complex reasoning, long-form content, and multi-modal tasks with images.

Best for:
- Detailed analysis and reasoning
- Long documents and content
- Image understanding and description

### **Gemini 2.5 Flash**
Fast and efficient variant of Gemini. Great for quick responses and simple tasks.

Best for:
- Rapid iterations
- Simple questions and quick answers
- Real-time content generation

### **GPT-5**
OpenAI's latest model. Balanced quality, creativity, and reasoning.

Best for:
- Creative writing and storytelling
- Balanced analysis and generation
- General-purpose tasks

### **GPT-4o-mini**
Lightweight and fast version of GPT. Cost-effective for simple tasks.

Best for:
- Quick responses
- Simple text generation
- Budget-conscious projects

💡 **Tip:** Use Gemini 2.5 Pro for complex tasks with images. Use Flash or GPT-4o-mini for quick, simple responses.

---

## 🎨 What to Expect

The AI will respond based on your prompt's clarity and detail.

Works best when you:
- Give clear instructions
- Specify format, length, or style
- Provide context or examples
- Ask one thing at a time

**Example good prompts:**
- "Write a cinematic image prompt for a sci-fi city at sunset"
- "Create 3 variations of social media copy for this product image (50 characters max)"
- "Describe this image in detail, focusing on lighting and composition"

**Less effective:**
- "Write something"
- "Help me"
- "What should I do?"

---

## 💬 Quick Tips

- Be specific about what you want (format, length, style)
- Use this node to **prepare prompts** for other generators
- Upload images if you want the AI to reference visual content
- Chain multiple Generate Text nodes to build complex ideas
- Different AI models have different strengths:
  - **Gemini 2.5 Pro** — best for complex reasoning and long content
  - **Gemini 2.5 Flash** — fastest for quick responses
  - **GPT-5** — balanced quality and creativity
  - **GPT-4o-mini** — lightweight and fast for simple tasks
