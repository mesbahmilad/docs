# Image to Video

Turn your favorite still into a living scene.  
This node takes a static image and adds motion — camera movement, subtle animation, or atmospheric effects. Perfect for making product shots dynamic or bringing illustrations to life.

---

## 🪄 When to Use It

Use Image to Video when you have a great still image and want to add movement.

It works great for:
- **Product animations** — add smooth camera pans around your product
- **Concept art in motion** — see your illustration come alive
- **Social media content** — eye-catching animated posts
- **Portfolio pieces** — make your work stand out with motion

**Skill level:** Intermediate  
**Average time:** 60–120 seconds per clip  
**Cost:** High

---

## 🎚️ Controls and Parameters

### **Prompt** (Text, Required)
Describe the motion you want to add. Focus on camera movement and subtle animations.

💡 **Tip:** "Slow camera pan to the right, soft bokeh in background" is clearer than "make it move."

### **Initial Image** (Image, Required)
The still image you want to animate. Works best with clean, well-composed images.

### **Background Audio** (Audio, Optional)
Add a soundtrack or ambient sound to accompany your animation.

### **Duration** (Select)
Choose clip length:
- **4 seconds** — quick motion, faster generation
- **6 seconds** — balanced for most uses
- **8 seconds** — longer, cinematic movements

💡 **Tip:** Longer durations work best with slow, smooth camera movements.

### **Resolution** (Select)
- **720p** — faster generation, preview quality
- **1080p** — higher quality for final delivery

### **Aspect Ratio** (Select)
- **16:9** — landscape format
- **9:16** — portrait format

💡 **Tip:** Match the aspect ratio to your source image for best results.

### **Generate Audio** (Toggle)
Automatically create ambient sound that matches the scene.

## 🎨 Available Models

Choose the model that fits your animation style:

### **Veo 3** (Default)
Google's advanced image-to-video model. Excellent at preserving image details while adding natural motion.

### **Wan2.5**
Versatile model with support for custom background audio and multiple resolutions (480p, 720p, 1080p).

### **Kling 2.5 Turbo**
Fast animation generation. Good for quick previews and social media content.

### **Sora 2**
Standard quality with reliable motion. Supports 4, 8, and 12-second durations.

### **Sora 2 [Pro]**
Premium version with better detail preservation and smoother motion.

💡 **Tip:** Veo 3 gives the most natural-looking animations. Try Kling Turbo for speed.

---

## 🎨 What to Expect

The AI will add motion while trying to preserve the essence of your image.  
Best results come from:
- **Clear, well-lit source images**
- **Simple camera movements** (pan, zoom, dolly)
- **Specific motion prompts** ("camera slowly moves forward")

The animation may introduce slight changes to details — that's normal. Think of this as bringing your image to life, not making an exact animated copy.

---

## 💬 Quick Tips

- Use high-quality source images for best results
- Describe the specific camera move you want (pan, tilt, zoom)
- Subtle motion often looks better than dramatic changes
- Match your prompt to the mood of the image
- Try multiple generations with different motion descriptions
