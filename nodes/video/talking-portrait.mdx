# Talking Portrait

Make any portrait speak.  
This node takes a still image of a face and syncs it to audio, creating natural lip movement and subtle facial animation. Perfect for bringing characters to life or creating talking avatars.

---

## 🪄 When to Use It

Use Talking Portrait when you want to animate a still face with speech.

It works great for:
- **Character dialogue** — make your illustrated characters speak
- **Avatar creation** — animated profile pictures or mascots
- **Story narration** — portraits that deliver voiceover
- **Explainer videos** — talking head style without filming

**Skill level:** Intermediate  
**Average time:** 30–60 seconds per clip  
**Cost:** Medium-High

---

## 🎚️ Controls and Parameters

### **Image** (Image, Required)
A portrait or character image with a visible face. Works best with:
- Clear frontal or 3/4 view of the face
- Good lighting on the face
- Minimal motion blur

💡 **Tip:** AI-generated portraits often work better than photos because they're cleaner and more consistent.

### **Audio** (Audio, Required)
The speech or dialogue you want the portrait to lip-sync to. Can be:
- Generated from Voice Generator node
- Uploaded audio file
- Connected from another audio node

### **Prompt** (Text, Required)
Describe the character and context — helps the AI understand the mood and style.

💡 **Tip:** "Professional woman speaking confidently to camera" or "Animated character telling a story" sets the tone.

### **Seed** (Number)
Control randomness. Same seed + same inputs = same result.

### **Intensity** (Slider, 0–1)
How much facial movement to add:
- **Low (0.3–0.5)** — subtle, natural movement
- **Medium (0.6–0.8)** — noticeable but realistic
- **High (0.9–1.0)** — expressive, animated style

💡 **Tip:** Start around 0.8 for most portraits. Lower for more realistic, higher for cartoon-like.

---

## 🎨 Available Models

### **Infinite Talk** (Default)
Advanced lip-sync technology optimized for portrait animation. Produces natural facial movement with accurate lip-sync to audio.

Supports:
- Variable intensity control
- Seed-based consistency
- Custom prompts for context

💡 **Tip:** Infinite Talk is currently the only model for this node and handles all portrait animation scenarios.

---

## 🎨 What to Expect

The AI will:
- Animate the mouth to match the audio
- Add subtle head movements and eye blinks
- Keep the overall character appearance consistent

Works best with:
- **Clear audio** — crisp dialogue without background noise
- **Forward-facing portraits** — frontal or slight angle views
- **Well-lit images** — shadows on the face can cause issues

Less predictable with:
- Profile views or extreme angles
- Multiple faces in one image (picks the main face)
- Very stylized or abstract art

---

## 💬 Quick Tips

- Generate portraits with Text to Image for most consistent results
- Use Voice Generator to create matching audio clips
- Keep intensity around 0.7-0.9 for natural-looking motion
- Audio length determines video length (longer audio = longer processing)
- Works with illustrated characters, not just realistic portraits
