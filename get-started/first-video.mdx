Welcome to video creation on Wundernode.  
Video feels complex, but it doesn't have to be. Start simple, experiment freely, and watch your ideas move.

---

## 🎬 Text to Video

Describe motion, get a clip.  
No filming, no footage — just type what you want to see happen, and AI generates it from scratch.

  allowFullScreen

💡 **Quick tip:** Describe camera movement explicitly — "camera slowly pans left over a futuristic city" works better than just "city scene."

💡 **Quick tip:** Start with 4-6 seconds. Longer clips take much more time to generate.

## 📸 Image to Video

Turn a still into motion.  
Upload a photo and add movement — perfect for product animations, character reveals, or bringing artwork to life.

💡 **Quick tip:** Simple movements work best — camera zooms, gentle pans, or subtle subject motion.

💡 **Quick tip:** High-quality input images produce higher-quality video output.

---

## 🎞️ First and Last Frame to Video

Control the beginning and end, let AI fill the middle.  
Upload two images — your start and finish — and watch AI create the smooth transition between them.

  width="100%"

⚠️ **Note:** This feature is currently only supported by the **VEO 3.1** model. Make sure to select VEO 3.1 in the model dropdown.

💡 **Quick tip:** Keep your first and last frames visually similar — small, logical changes work better than dramatic transformations.

💡 **Quick tip:** This is perfect for smooth morphs, subtle transitions, and controlled storytelling moments.
