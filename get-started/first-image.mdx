## 🎨 Text to Image

Start with words, end with a picture.  
Type what you see in your mind, hit generate, and watch it appear. Perfect when you're starting from scratch.

## 🖌️ Single Image Edit

Already have an image? Make it better.  
Upload a photo (or use the image you just created), describe what you want to change, and let AI handle the heavy lifting. Perfect for quick tweaks and creative experiments.

  width="100%"

💡 **Quick tip:** You can change colors, add objects, remove backgrounds, or completely reimagine the style — all with natural language.

💡 **Quick tip:** The more specific your edit prompt, the better. Try "change the sky to dramatic sunset" instead of just "better sky."

## 🎨 Multi Image Edit

Combine multiple images into one cohesive scene.  
Upload several photos and blend them together — perfect for product photography, composite scenes, or merging different elements into a single frame.
