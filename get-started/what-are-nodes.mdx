When you think, you don't think in a straight line.  
Your mind jumps from an idea to an image, to a memory, to a tweak — and somehow it all connects into something that feels right.

Most AI tools, however, **force you into a single prompt box.**  
You type. You wait. You hope. Then you start over.

That's not how creativity works.

---

## Why Nodes Exist

**Wundernode is built on the belief that creativity is nonlinear.**

Artists explore. We test an idea, branch it, loop it back, mix it with something else.  
Nodes are the digital form of that process — a way to **visually connect thoughts**, experiment freely, and build complex creations step by step.

Each node is one piece of your thinking:
- "What if I add more realism here?"
- "Can I make this more artistic?"
- "Maybe this mood should flow into the next part?"

Instead of rewriting prompts endlessly, you just **connect ideas together**.  
The output of one thought flows into the next — like neurons firing in a creative brain.

---

## The Main Types of Nodes

### 🎨 Generators
These nodes make something new from text, images, or ideas.

**Image Generators:**
- **Text to Image** — turn words into pictures
- **Image Editor** — blend and remix existing images with natural language

**Video Generators:**
- **Text to Video** — create video clips from descriptions
- **Image to Video** — animate still images
- **Talking Portrait** — make portraits speak with audio

**Audio & Text:**
- **Voice Generator** — clone voices and create speech from text
- **Generate Text** — use AI to write, brainstorm, and expand ideas

---

### 🧰 Enhancers
These nodes take existing content and make it better.

**Image Enhancement:**
- **Image Upscale** — increase resolution while preserving detail

**Video Enhancement:**
- **Lip Sync** — replace dialogue in videos
- **Frame Interpolation** — smooth motion to 60fps

They help you polish without starting over.

---

### 📥 Inputs
These nodes let you add your own files to the canvas.

- **Text Display** — type prompts and notes directly
- **Image Input** — upload or drag-drop photos
- **Video Input** — bring your own footage
- **Audio Input** — upload files or record directly

💡 **Tip:** Just drag and drop files onto the canvas — Wundernode automatically creates the right input node!

They connect your existing work to AI tools.

## Connecting Thoughts

A workflow isn't a rigid recipe — it's a **map of your thinking**.

```
Text Display → Text to Image → Image Editor → Image Upscale
```

Each arrow says "take this and build on it."

You can branch paths, merge results, or re-route them entirely.  
It's creative thinking, visualized — a sandbox where curiosity has no penalty.

Prompts are quick, but they're one-dimensional.  
Nodes let you **see your ideas take shape** — visually, logically, and playfully.  

You can branch off a version, experiment, and loop back — just like you do in your sketchbook or when you're composing music.

That's the magic of nodes:  
They don't just make AI easier to control —  
they make it **feel human again.**
